The Vestas Annual Report 2025 is a 195-page annual report, not a simple text-only PDF. 
It contains multiple major document sections, including management commentary, sustainability disclosures, financial statements, notes, risk management and auditor statements.

It includes several document patterns that are useful for parser research:

- narrative paragraphs,
- multi-column page layouts,
- financial and sustainability tables,
- charts and visual indicators,
- images with captions,
- repeated headers and footers,
- page numbers,
- footnotes,
- section hierarchies,
- financial figures using parentheses for negative values,
- special characters and symbols,
- content distributed across multiple pages.

For example, the financial key-figure page contains several logically separate tables for financial ratios, share ratios, operational figures, income statements, balance-sheet figures and cash flows.

The sustainability section also includes structured metrics, units, year-wise values and extensive footnotes. This makes it useful for evaluating whether numerical values, labels and their relationships survive extraction.

The report also contains risk-oriented structures such as a double-materiality assessment, where impacts, risks and opportunities are organised visually and elaborated through subsequent tables.
